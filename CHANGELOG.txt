# Changelog

All notable changes to the gateway of nas-os are documented in this file.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project
uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Security

- `X-NAS-Secure: 1` goes with a request that came over TLS or from the monitor of the NAS, and one
  sent by the browser is dropped: the launcher asks it before it takes or gives back a password or a
  key.

### Added

- The computers of the desktop client NAS Sync: `POST /auth/clients`, with the password
  confirmed, issues a certificate in PEM for the identity of their sync engine; `/client/sync/...`
  reaches the client routes of nas-sync-manager only with that certificate, naming the computer in
  `x-nas-client` and starting the application when it does not answer; a certificate pairs once.
  `x-nas-client` is dropped from every other request. Revoking or removing such a computer drops
  its sync engine from nas-sync-manager, retried every five minutes until it succeeds (migration
  `0005_sync_clients.sql`). Untested on a machine.
- First demo release of the nas-gateway.
